# Designing a professional brand

A brand isn't just a logo. It's the impression people get from your name, visuals, words and behaviour. Consistency is what makes small businesses look professional.

## The brand foundation

Answer these in writing:

- **Purpose:** Why does this business exist?
- **Audience:** Who is it for?
- **Promise:** What can customers count on?
- **Personality:** Three adjectives (for example: clear, friendly, practical).
- **Values:** What will you always or never do?

## Name

Choose a name that is easy to spell, easy to say and not too similar to existing brands. Check domain availability and search trademark registers in your country before committing. A trademark search is not optional if you plan to grow.

## Colour

Use a simple palette:

- 1 primary colour
- 1 accent colour
- 1 dark neutral and 1 light neutral

Check that text has enough contrast with its background; pale text on pale backgrounds is the most common mistake. Aim for contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for body text.

## Typography

Pick **two fonts**: one for headings and one for body text. Sans-serif fonts such as Inter, Montserrat or Poppins read well on screens. Keep a few sizes (heading, subheading, body, caption) and use them consistently.

## Imagery and layout

- Use plenty of white space; crowded designs look amateur.
- Limit text on each graphic to one main message.
- Align elements to a grid and keep margins consistent.
- Use real photography or consistent illustration styles, and always check licences.

## Voice

Write down how you speak: formal or casual, playful or calm, short or detailed. Create 5 example sentences in your voice and 5 phrases you'll avoid.

## Make a mini brand kit

Put it all on one page: logo, colours with hex codes, fonts, voice summary and example post. In Canva, save the colours and fonts in a brand kit if your plan offers it, or simply keep a document.

## Exercise

Create your one-page brand kit and apply it to three posts. Ask someone if the posts look like they come from the same brand.

**Checklist:** ☐ Foundation written ☐ Name checked ☐ Palette set ☐ Fonts chosen ☐ Voice defined ☐ Brand kit page

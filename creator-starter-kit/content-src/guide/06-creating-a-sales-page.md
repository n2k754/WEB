# Creating a sales page

A sales page helps a visitor decide. Its job is to answer questions clearly, not to pressure. The best pages are honest, specific and easy to scan.

## The five-block structure

1. **Headline and promise:** state the outcome and who it's for.
2. **The problem:** show you understand the situation in the visitor's words.
3. **The solution:** introduce the product in one or two sentences.
4. **What's inside:** list the exact contents, formats and quantities.
5. **Call to action:** one clear button with the price.

## Write for clarity

- Replace vague claims ("amazing quality") with facts ("30 ideas, each with a hook and structure").
- Use short paragraphs and descriptive headings.
- Show the product: screenshots, sample pages or a short preview.
- Explain how delivery works and what customers need (for example, a free account on a given tool).

## Build trust honestly

- Show the real person or business behind it and a way to contact you.
- Link to terms, privacy, delivery and refund information.
- Use testimonials only if they are real and you have permission. Never fabricate reviews, numbers or "as seen on" logos.
- Be clear about limitations. It reduces refunds and complaints.

## Pricing section

Show the price in the currency your customers use, say whether taxes are included, and list what each option contains. If you have tiers, make the differences easy to compare. A recommended option is fine, as long as the claim is truthful.

## FAQ

Answer questions such as who it's for, what's included, how files are delivered, what's the refund policy and whether commercial use is allowed.

## Technical basics

- Make the page fast and readable on mobile first.
- Use a reputable payment provider; never handle card data yourself.
- Deliver files only after payment is confirmed server-side.
- Add a clear confirmation page and an email address for support.

## Exercise

Write the five blocks in plain text before designing anything. Read it aloud. If a sentence could describe any product, make it more specific.

**Checklist:** ☐ Five blocks written ☐ Exact contents listed ☐ Legal links added ☐ FAQ written ☐ Mobile tested

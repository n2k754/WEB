# Marketing through TikTok and Instagram

Short-form platforms can introduce your work to people who have never heard of you. Results vary widely and no method guarantees growth. Think in terms of experiments, learning and consistency.

## Set up your profile

- **Name and handle:** easy to read and consistent across platforms.
- **Bio:** who you help, what result you offer, one call to action.
- **Link:** one clear destination (your sales page or a simple landing page).
- **Pinned content:** your best introduction and your best educational post.

## Content pillars

Choose 3 or 4 recurring themes, for example:

1. **Educate:** tips, tutorials, mistakes to avoid.
2. **Relate:** your story, behind the scenes, lessons learned.
3. **Prove:** examples, before and afters, real customer questions.
4. **Offer:** what you sell and who it's for.

A balanced mix prevents your feed from being only promotion.

## Hooks and structure for short videos

A useful structure: **Hook (first 2 seconds) → Value (one point) → Call to action.** Keep videos focused on a single idea. Test different opening lines for the same topic and compare watch time.

## Instagram formats

- **Reels:** discovery and reach.
- **Carousels:** education and saves.
- **Stories:** relationship and daily updates.
- **Captions:** context, keywords and one call to action.

## A realistic weekly rhythm

- 3 educational posts
- 1 personal or story-driven post
- 1 offer-related post
- 10 to 15 minutes engaging with replies and comments each day

Batch your creation once a week and schedule where possible.

## Measure what matters

Track weekly: average watch time, saves, shares, profile visits and link clicks. Look for patterns after at least several posts, not just one. Keep notes in your content tracker.

## Follow platform rules and the law

- Respect each platform's community guidelines and advertising policies.
- Disclose sponsored content or affiliate links as required where you live.
- Use music and images you have the right to use.
- Don't make income claims you can't substantiate.

## Exercise

Plan seven days of content with the pillars, write hooks for each, film in one batch and review the numbers after one week.

**Checklist:** ☐ Profile updated ☐ Pillars chosen ☐ 7 hooks ☐ Batch session ☐ Weekly review scheduled

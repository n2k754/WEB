# Creating a first digital product

Your first product doesn't need to be perfect or large. It needs to be **useful, clear and finished**. A finished simple product teaches you more than an unfinished ambitious one.

## Choose a format

| Format | Best for | Tools to start |
|---|---|---|
| Template pack | Audiences who want speed | Canva, Figma, Notion |
| PDF guide | Teaching a process | A word processor, Canva, Google Docs |
| Checklist or workbook | Helping people take action | Notion, Canva |
| Spreadsheet | Tracking and planning | A spreadsheet app |
| Mini-course | Step-by-step skills | Slides, recorded video |

Pick the format that your audience will use fastest.

## Outline before you create

1. Write the **result**: "After using this, the customer can ____."
2. List the 5 to 8 steps that lead to that result.
3. Turn each step into a section with a short explanation, an example and an action.
4. Decide what's out of scope. Saying "this product doesn't cover X" is healthy.

## Build in a weekend (a realistic schedule)

- **Day 1 morning:** outline and gather facts. Verify anything you state as fact.
- **Day 1 afternoon:** write or build the main content.
- **Day 2 morning:** add examples, checklists and design.
- **Day 2 afternoon:** test with 2 or 3 people and fix confusing parts.

For larger products, split the work into one-week blocks.

## Quality checklist

- Is the promise specific and honest?
- Can a beginner follow it without extra help?
- Are files named clearly and organised in folders?
- Did you proofread the text and test every link, template or formula?
- Does it include a short "how to use this" page?
- Are you allowed to use every font, image and tool you included? Check licences.

## Rights and licences

Decide what customers may do: personal use, commercial use, editing, resale. State it in a plain-language licence file. Don't copy others' work, and don't promise Canva or Notion features you can't deliver.

## Exercise

Write your outcome sentence, create your outline and set a finish date within 14 days.

**Checklist:** ☐ Format chosen ☐ Outline done ☐ First draft ☐ Test with 3 people ☐ Licence written

# Start here

Thank you for purchasing the CREATOR STARTER KIT. This folder contains everything in your package, organised by component.

## Suggested order

1. Read the **Digital Business Guide** (04) to choose and shape your idea.
2. Browse the **TikTok content ideas** (02) and pick your first three videos.
3. Open the **templates** (01) and follow the how-to guide.
4. If your package includes them: set up the **Notion dashboard** (03), copy the **AI prompts** (05), and complete the **brand workbook** (06) and **content planning guide** (07).

# Bonus: content planning guide

A content plan turns scattered ideas into a rhythm you can maintain. This guide works with the Notion dashboard but can be used with any tool.

## 1. Define your goal

Choose one primary goal for the next 30 days: awareness (more people see you), trust (people learn from you) or action (people visit your page). One goal keeps your content focused.

## 2. Choose 3 or 4 content pillars

Pillars are recurring themes. A simple set: **Educate**, **Relate**, **Prove**, **Offer**. Write 10 topic ideas under each pillar before you plan any dates.

## 3. Pick a sustainable frequency

Consistency beats intensity. Choose a number you can maintain even during a busy week (for example three posts per week) and increase only when it feels easy.

## 4. The four-week plan

| Week | Theme | Content mix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduce yourself and your topic | 2 educate, 1 relate |
| 2 | Teach one core skill | 2 educate, 1 prove |
| 3 | Share a story and examples | 1 educate, 1 relate, 1 prove |
| 4 | Introduce your offer | 1 educate, 1 offer, 1 relate |

## 5. Batching routine (2 hours)

1. 20 min: choose topics and write hooks.
2. 30 min: write scripts or captions.
3. 45 min: film or design everything.
4. 15 min: edit and schedule.
5. 10 min: record everything in your tracker.

## 6. Repurposing map

One idea → short video → carousel → story poll → caption → email. Note the original idea ID in your tracker so you can see what performed across formats.

## 7. Weekly review (15 minutes)

Record views, saves, shares, profile visits and link clicks. Ask: what worked? What surprised me? What will I test next? Keep decisions small and reversible.

## 8. Content quality checklist

☐ One clear idea ☐ A strong first line ☐ One call to action ☐ Facts checked ☐ Rights for music and images ☐ Accessibility: captions or alt text

## 9. When you fall behind

Don't try to catch up. Skip a day, reuse a top-performing idea, or post a simpler format. Protect the habit.

## 10. 30-day template

Use the Weekly Planning and Content Calendar databases from your Notion dashboard. Add each post with a date, pillar, format and status, then move it through *Idea → Drafting → Ready → Published*.

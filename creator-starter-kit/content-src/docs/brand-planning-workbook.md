# Brand planning workbook

Use this workbook after reading chapter 5 of the Digital Business Guide. Print it or copy the sections into Notion. Write in pencil first: brands evolve.

## Part 1 · Brand foundation

**1. Purpose.** Complete: "We exist to help ______ achieve ______ by ______."

**2. Audience.** Describe one real person you want to help (situation, goal, struggle).

**3. Promise.** In one sentence, what can customers always count on from you?

**4. Values.** Choose three and write one behaviour for each.

## Part 2 · Positioning

Fill in the statement: "For **[audience]** who **[need]**, **[brand]** is the **[category]** that **[key benefit]**. Unlike **[alternative]**, we **[difference]**."

Then list three proof points you can honestly back up (features, process, experience). Don't list claims you can't support.

## Part 3 · Voice and tone

Choose your position on each scale (1 to 5):

| Formal ↔ Casual | 1 2 3 4 5 |
|---|---|
| Serious ↔ Playful | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| Technical ↔ Simple | 1 2 3 4 5 |
| Reserved ↔ Enthusiastic | 1 2 3 4 5 |

Write three phrases you'll use and three you'll avoid.

## Part 5 · Naming and handles

List 10 name options. Cross out any that are hard to spell, too similar to existing brands or already taken as a domain or handle. Check trademark registers in your country. Final shortlist: ______ , ______ , ______.

## Part 6 · Customer journey

Describe each step and what the customer needs:

1. **Discover:** where do they first see you?
2. **Trust:** what makes them stay?
3. **Decide:** which questions must the sales page answer?
4. **Buy:** what must checkout make clear (price, taxes, delivery)?
5. **Use:** how do they get results quickly?
6. **Return:** how will you ask for honest feedback?
